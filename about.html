Our Story

Considered Craftsmanship

Founded in Ireland, WearBears creates clothing for men and women who value quiet luxury over fleeting trends. Every piece is designed in-house and produced in small batches using fine natural fibres — wool, silk, cashmere, full-grain leather — chosen to age well and last for years.

We work with a small number of family-run mills and workshops across Europe, favouring craft and durability over speed. It's a slower way of making clothes, but one we believe is worth it.
